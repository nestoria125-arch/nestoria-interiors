Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request a design consultation, cost estimation, or catalog, we collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, contact phone number, email address, property configuration, and site location in the Pune/PCMC region.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you regarding your interior design requirements and floor plan discussions.
  • To schedule site visits and studio consultations at our Chinchwad facility.
  • To transmit customized 3D design proposals, Bill of Quantities (BOQs), and project updates.

3. Data Sharing & Protection

Nestoria does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Data is handled securely and only accessible by authorized design consultants and project managers responsible for your installation.

If you have any questions regarding your personal information, contact us at contact@nestoriakitchen.com or call +91 8446714207.
